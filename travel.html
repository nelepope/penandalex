Travel

3 July 2027 · Witherenden Farm House, East Sussex

By train from London

The nearest station is Stonegate, conveniently on a direct train line from Charing Cross and London Bridge. Trains run every hour and take about 1 hour 15 minutes. Witherenden is a 10 minute walk from the station along the road. Warning: there is no pavement.

Note: Other than the station, Witherenden is not near anything else. It is not in a city or a town or a village. There are no buses or pubs within walking distance.
